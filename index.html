About

Morgan Wallen sets the pace in, and beyond, country music – with Billboard calling him “one of the biggest stars in the music world right now.” With 21 No. 1 singles at Country radio, 19 Billboard Music Awards to-date, and more than 225 weeks spent atop Billboard’s Top Country Albums chart.
